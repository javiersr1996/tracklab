## Orientation and court geometry (task 9)

Generated by `tracklab orientation` from the bronze Parquet (never the raw files). Every figure below is read from `pipeline/reports/orientation_basketball.csv` and `pipeline/reports/orientation_football.csv`, one row per match, and this section is rendered from those rows. The table `bronze.common.orientation` (`pipeline/data/bronze/common/orientation/data.parquet`, columns `sport, native_match_id, period, native_team_id, attack_sign, evidence`) is what `dim_team_period` (task 10) reads: `attack_sign = +1` when the team attacks towards +x of the venue frame in that period, `-1` otherwise, and the two teams of a period always carry opposite signs.

### Decision 2 of the silver schema, as measured

Silver keeps the **venue frame** of the tracking in both sports (origin at the centre of the surface, x along the length, as the cameras deliver it) and stores the attack direction as a **sign per (match, period, team)**, never baked into `frames`. The events of both sports arrive in an **attack frame** and are mapped to the venue frame by a 180-degree rotation chosen by a provider field: basketball `(x, y)` when `possessions.leftHoop` is true, `(-x, -y)` otherwise (attacked hoop at negative x); football `(x, y)` when `attacking_side` is `left_to_right`, `(-x, -y)` when `right_to_left` (attacker towards +x). The sections below hold the per-match evidence.

### Basketball: `possessions.leftHoop` gives the sign, the shot probe checks it

In the 10 matches every (period, team) group shares `leftHoop` across all its possessions and the two teams of a period hold opposite values (`left_hoop_consensus` = `ok` in every row; a mixed group fails the run, it is never averaged). `attack_sign = -1` when `leftHoop` is true (the offence attacks the hoop at negative x of the venue frame), `+1` otherwise. Periods per match: 4 to 5 (overtime is a fifth period).

**Shot probe.** The shooter's `tracking_objects` position at `shots.startFrame`, rotated into the attack frame of the events by the possession's `leftHoop` (`(x, y)` when true, `(-x, -y)` when false), against `shots.location`. Both sides are in feet as delivered and the tolerance is 0.5 m = 1.6404 ft. `flipped` is the opposite rotation. **What decides the question is the comparison, not a percentage**: the rule wins when the opposite rotation matches no shot at all and the rule's own median residual sits inside the tolerance. A rotation flipped anywhere can satisfy neither, which a share threshold could not guarantee. Shots without a location, without a possession or without a shooter position at that frame are counted and excluded from the denominator.
